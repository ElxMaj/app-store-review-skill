# Guideline 2.3.7 rejection recovery

Use the `app-store-review` skill to diagnose the rejection in
`inputs/rejection-details.md` and draft a concise Resolution Center reply.

Treat the supplied rejection as the complete case record. Do not invent an app
name, developer identity, submission identifier, metadata localization, binary
change, attachment, version, or build. State what must be verified before
resubmission when the supplied evidence does not establish it.
