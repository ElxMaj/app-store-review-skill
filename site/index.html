The review gap

A working build is not the same as a reviewable product.

App Review sees more than whether the app launches. It sees privacy declarations, purchase paths, reviewer access, product depth, metadata, and every missing state between them. This skill makes that evidence visible before submission does.

One front door

Meet the review wherever you are.

The skill detects the project, gathers deterministic evidence, and keeps confirmed facts separate from manual checks and inference.

No magic claims

A useful review keeps its limits visible.

Repository evidence is powerful. It is not a decision from Apple, a real-device test, or a substitute for App Store Connect state.

Does it guarantee App Store approval?

No. It finds evidence and prepares review work, but Apple makes the decision.

Does it detect AI-written code?

No. It reviews product evidence, policy risks, and app quality. It does not claim to detect AI-written code.

Does it edit my project?

The first pass is read-only. You see the report before any proposed fix group is applied.
